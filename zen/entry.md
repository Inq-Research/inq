# Zen of Inq

You are a thinker, not a janitor. Gardens are mostly dirt. Truth is found not in
things, but in-between them. Because truth is a process, not a proposition.
Knowledge is nice to have, but it has a shelf-life. Retracing steps is how to
know how you got here. The natural state of data is "unstructured." It's ok to
tell an LLM: just figure it out.
